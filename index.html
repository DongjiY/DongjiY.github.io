A place for my thoughts

I remember drawing lots of doodles in my notebook during school. Looking back on my notes was always entertaining, but quite useless towards the purpose of studying.

Since my work these days requires less pen and paper, it's become harder to find an outlet for these doodles.

As a result, I've decided to convert this personal site into my new notebook: A place to express my thoughts and share my lackluster art with any soul who stumbles upon this site. I hope you enjoy.

Symphony Previews

I keep an ever-growing list of concerts that interest me. For each concert, I clip short highlights of the pieces to convince others to attend. You can find this list here.

I play the viola in my free time, but not nearly as well as I would like (much to the dismay of my neighbors). Until then, the symphony scratches my itch.

I've particularly enjoyed performances by the Seattle Symphony, although I recognize that I may be biased because they are my home orchestra.

L2-Table

L2 table is an exploratory project that I am currently working on. The goal of L2 table is to create a performant canvas-based table that reduces the time taken by the browser's layouting phase. This makes it a suitable candidate for displaying dense, rapidly-updating datasets.

The interactive demo below uses the published npm package. Its source code can be found on my github.

The interactive L2-table demo is available on desktop screens.

Demo data is mocked and does not reflect live market prices.
