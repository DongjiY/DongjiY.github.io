Commentary

Capriccio Espagnole is one of my favorite pieces. The work depicts various folk tunes from Spain. Funnily enough, Rimsky Korsakov had never visited the country; all of the tunes were based on available published music at the time. Korsakov originally intended for this work to be a violin solo, but opted instead to use it as a showcase for the entire orchestra. You can hear solos from violin, clarinet, and percussion (among others).
