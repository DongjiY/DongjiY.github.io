Commentary

Rachmaninoff's second piano concerto is a cornerstone of the romantic era. Although most people can recognize its sweeping melodies, lesser known is the history behind the work.

Rachmaninoff was in a deep depression after the failed premier of his first symphony. He faced a crippling creative block for years afterwards. However, with the help of his therapist, Dr. Nikolai Dahl, Rachmaninoff was able to overcome his adversity and complete his second piano concerto. The piece was first performed in Moscow, 1901, to great critical acclaim.

The piano concerto is dedicated to Dr. Nikolai Dahl as a gesture of gratitude for his part in helping Rachmaninoff recover from his depression.
