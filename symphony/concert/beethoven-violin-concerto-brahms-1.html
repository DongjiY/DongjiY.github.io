Commentary

Brahms' first symphony took the composer around two decades to complete. The long delay can be attributed to a combination of his meticulous attention to detail and the enormous legacy left behind by Beethoven, whose 9 groundbreaking symphonies bridged the end of the classical period and ushered in the romantic era.

With such a large burden on his shoulder, Brahms wanted to ensure that he could produce a work that was worthy of its place amongst his peers and to himself.

Although I find his symphony no. 1 to contain less flair, it undeniably contains the rich and beautiful melodies associated with romanic era pieces.
